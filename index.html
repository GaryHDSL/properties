Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

Is it free to submit a property request?

This demonstration website does not charge customers to submit a request. Any future fees or paid services must be clearly disclosed before launch.

How do estate agents join?

Agents can submit the registration form. A live platform will need account verification, secure login and an administrator approval process.

Are the properties shown available?

The displayed cards are illustrative examples, not verified live listings. Availability, pricing and property details must be confirmed by the listing professional.

Can landlords list directly?

Yes. The planned platform supports landlord and seller submissions, subject to identity checks and listing review.

How are enquiries matched?

In the live version, matching can use location, property type, budget and the service areas selected by participating professionals.

Can I manage my enquiries online?

The interface previews a lead-management service. A backend, database and notification service must be connected to activate it.
